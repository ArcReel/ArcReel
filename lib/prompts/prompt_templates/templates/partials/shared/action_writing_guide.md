The first frame already fixes the subjects, setting, and style; this text drives it into motion. Describe only movement and change during the specified duration, without repeating static visual content. Camera movement belongs exclusively in camera_motion; action describes only subject and environmental movement. Write connected narrative sentences in layers: subject action (body / gestures / expression transitions), object interaction (stroking letter paper, air stirred by opening a door, etc.), and environmental dynamics (hems, dust, rain, moving light and shadow). Verbs should describe physically observable actions (reach / turn / stroke / cast toward / tighten), avoiding mental verbs. Favor slow, continuous subtle action, matching the amount of action to shot duration: a roughly 5-second shot usually completes one coherent action plus one detailed interaction; an 8-second shot can carry one action transition (such as “raise head, meet eyes, begin speaking”); longer shots keep one slow action thread, increasing the number of action stages with duration (such as “stand up, walk to the window, stop”), rather than layering several parallel actions.
   Good: “Lin Qing slowly raises her head, the corners of her eyes tighten slightly, and her fingers absent-mindedly stroke the edge of the letter paper; outside the window the rain grows heavier, and shadows of rain streaks move slowly across the tabletop.” Subject action, object interaction, and environmental dynamics each receive a detail.
   Bad: “Lin Qing flutters like a butterfly, her thoughts rapidly switching between past and present.” “Thoughts switching” is not filmable movement; “like a butterfly” is figurative language, not an action description.
   Avoid task-type trigger words: do not use imperative verbs such as “add / delete / remove / modify / replace / change to / extend / continue writing”. Some models infer task type from prompt wording; these words can cause reference-video generation to be mistaken for video editing or extension. Describe the intended scene directly: instead of “change the coat to red”, write “she is wearing a red coat”.
